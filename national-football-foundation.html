Honoring Vermont Football

National Football Foundation — Vermont Chapter

Founded in 1947, the National Football Foundation & College Hall of Fame is a national nonprofit that uses the power of amateur football to build scholarship, citizenship, and athletic achievement in young people. The Vermont Chapter recognizes standout Vermont high school and college players, coaches, officials, and community members every year at its annual Hall of Fame Banquet.
