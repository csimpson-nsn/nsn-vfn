Vermont Football History

Hall of Champions

Every VIFL state championship by division, year, and score. Vermont ran a four-division playoff structure (Divisions I–IV) through the mid-2000s before consolidating to the current three-division format.

This list covers 1995–2025 (100 of 159 total championships on record). Earlier years going back further exist in the historical archive but aren't included here yet — if you have those results (program records, VPA archives, newspaper clippings), send them over and we'll add them. Source: vermontprepfootball.com/state-champs.
